Changelog
=========

Unreleased
----------

Added
~~~~~

- Prayer times calculated on the device with adhan-js (ISNA, Hanafi Asr by
  default; all common methods, high-latitude rules and per-prayer
  adjustments).
- Calligraphy theme: per-period photos, gold calligraphy, countdown pill,
  current/next prayer dial, sunrise/sunset and weather medallions.
- Classic theme: clock card, prayer cards, countdown medallion with progress
  arc, geometric patterns, verse/hadith/dua of the day.
- Kids theme: sun, clouds, birds, grass, flowers and a mosque, with rotating
  reminders.
- Night theme: dim drifting clock with Fajr and last-third-of-night times;
  backlight dimming under Fully Kiosk Browser.
- Athan sequence: one-minute countdown, athan screen, dua after the athan,
  final iqamah countdown; Tahajjud athan option.
- Jumu'ah, Duha, sunrise and makruh-time handling; Ramadan suhoor and iftar
  countdowns and the iftar dua.
- Settings panel with location search, uploads for athans and photos, and
  previews of every sequence.
- Bundled athans (Alafasy, Makkah; regular and Fajr) and verified Quran and
  hadith texts, each generated by a script.
- Offline support (service worker precache) and automatic updates outside
  athan sequences.
- GitHub Actions: unit tests, build, Playwright e2e, keyless deploy to
  Firebase Hosting.
