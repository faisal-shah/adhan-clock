Changelog
=========

Unreleased
----------

Added
~~~~~

- Prayer times calculated on the device with adhan-js (ISNA, Hanafi Asr by
  default; all common methods, high-latitude rules and per-prayer
  adjustments).
- Calligraphy theme: per-period photos, gold calligraphy, countdown pill,
  current/next prayer dial, sunrise/sunset and weather medallions.
- Classic theme: clock card, prayer cards, countdown medallion with progress
  arc, geometric patterns, verse/hadith/dua of the day.
- Kids theme: sun, clouds, birds, grass, flowers and a mosque, with rotating
  reminders.
- Gallery theme: 52 captioned, calendar-style photographs (mosques,
  manuscripts and madrasas, museum treasures, lands of Islam), with the
  prayer times along the foot; museum objects shown whole over a blurred
  copy of themselves.
- Thuluth calligraphy artwork (KHATTAATT, used with permission) for the
  prayer names, rendered as gold leaf or ink; Ruqaa, Nastaliq and Kufi
  font styles remain selectable and cover words without artwork.
- Eid, Ramadan and Jumu'ah greetings in calligraphy; calligraphy pieces in
  the Gallery.
- "Calm" motion setting for slower tablets.
- Night theme: dim drifting clock with Fajr and last-third-of-night times;
  backlight dimming under Fully Kiosk Browser.
- Athan sequence: one-minute countdown, athan screen, dua after the athan,
  final iqamah countdown; Tahajjud athan option.
- Jumu'ah, Duha, sunrise and makruh-time handling; Ramadan suhoor and iftar
  countdowns and the iftar dua.
- Settings panel with location search and uploads for athans and photos.
- One photo library for the Gallery theme and the backgrounds: each part of
  the day rotates through any set of library photos, chosen in a full-screen
  picker or from the photo viewer; uploaded photos join the library and the
  Gallery.
- Background picker: *Select all* for every photo (including photos added
  later), *Deselect all*, and both for each group; with nothing chosen the
  suggested photos show. Parts of the day that
  share photos show different ones on the same day; Settings → Backgrounds
  lists each set from today's photo on.
- Preview: jump to any athan sequence, part of the day, Jumu'ah, Ramadan or
  Eid on real dates (with 60x fast-forward), and browse every theme, photo
  and calligraphy piece full screen.
- Bundled athans (Alafasy, Makkah; regular and Fajr) and verified Quran and
  hadith texts, each generated by a script.
- Offline support (service worker precache) and automatic updates outside
  athan sequences.
- GitHub Actions: unit tests, build, Playwright e2e, keyless deploy to
  Firebase Hosting.

Fixed
~~~~~

- Choosing a daytime theme during the calligraphy time after an athan had
  no visible effect until that time ended; it now ends the calligraphy time
  and shows the chosen theme. Settings → Display says when the calligraphy
  or night theme is covering the daytime theme, and until when.
- Ending a preview no longer brings back an athan screen or calligraphy
  time that had been closed before the preview.
- A preview no longer silences that prayer's real chime and athan later in
  the day; a preview left running ends itself after 20 minutes.
- Closing Settings during the athan no longer stops it, and pressing Play
  on a sound while the athan plays no longer cuts it off.
- The × on the countdown, dua and iqamah screens, and *Stop the athan*,
  close only that screen; closing the countdown used to cancel the athan.
- A stopped athan stays stopped after a reload or an update, and updates
  wait until Settings, the viewer and previews are closed.
- An athan stopped while its recording was still loading no longer starts
  afterwards; volume changes reach a sound that is already playing.
- An uploaded athan that can't be read falls back to the default athan
  instead of silence; failed media loads are retried.
- Uploads are only reported saved once stored (a full disk lost them
  silently); sounds pointing at a lost upload return to their defaults.
- Clearing a Jumu'ah time keeps the previous one; out-of-range numbers in
  stored settings are clamped; a browser that refuses to save settings no
  longer shows a blank page.
- No false time-zone warning for zones with two names (Asia/Kolkata and
  Asia/Calcutta); stale weather is hidden after three hours.
- Adding several photos at once no longer stops at the first unreadable
  file, and names the files it skipped.
- Prayer days follow the place's calendar, so a tablet set to a far-off
  zone no longer crashes; Isha after midnight no longer shows Tahajjud
  before it; places inside the polar circles get times on every day.
- The daily verse, Gallery order and background rotation no longer repeat
  or skip a day at a clock change in zones on UTC+0 in winter.
- A background opened full screen showed the current time's clock, prayer
  dial and countdown under another prayer's name; it now shows its own
  part of today (a Fajr photo at 5:16 AM, with the Fajr dial).
